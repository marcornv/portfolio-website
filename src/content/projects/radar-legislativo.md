A free dashboard for following what happens in Brazil's Congress (the Câmara dos Deputados and the Senado Federal), using only official public data. It runs on your own computer.

- **Who's in Congress:** seats by party, members per state and a filterable list of politicians.
- **Votes:** results from the last 7, 30 or 90 days and upcoming sessions, with how every politician and party voted.
- **Bills in progress:** grouped by stage, with a timeline and the votes for each bill.
- **Weekly agenda:** sessions and committee meetings in both houses.
- **Politician profiles:** voting record, attendance, bills written, budget amendments, office expenses and committees.
- **Budget amendments:** who directed public money, where it went and how much has been paid out.
- **Plain-language summaries (optional):** AI explains each bill in simple terms, using Claude, ChatGPT, Groq or a free AI that runs entirely on your computer.
- **Easy to set up:** one Docker command, with a settings screen that explains and checks each optional key.
- **Private by default:** runs only on your machine. MIT licensed.
